11. GImageView History

2026-11-06: Version 0.2.27+gtk4 (GTK 4 version), changes since 2026-09-26

  • Bookmarks of images (~/.gimv/bookmarks), in the index or not: [Edit | Bookmark...] and the right button menus (no key by default, one can be given in ~/.gimv/keyaccelrc), an optional note, the "Bookmarks" tab (by the day added, with or without a note), the note in the tooltip; they follow the images moved or renamed.

  • Edit Tags: an entry with the tags below it (x takes one away), the tags in use to click, the names of people kept; also in [Edit | Edit Tags...] and the right button menus of the preview and the image window.

  • The tags of the Data tab and of Edit Tags laid out as words (no wide gaps). A row of the Data tab with a key not edited there (X-IMG-Tags, ...) shows its own key and is only shown. A line break in a tag or a value (a pasted text) becomes a space.

  • The "Date" of the comment is the date of the image (before EXIF; for images of SNS).

  • "Date taken" column of the detail view and View > Sort File List > by Date Taken (the date of the index; Reverse Order for the newest first).

  • After a sort (or a change of the display mode) the thumbnails made first are the ones now at the top.

  • Data tab: a value typed is kept by "Save" without Enter.

  • Properties: long names and types cut (the dialog stays on the screen), selectable; "Copy" for the full path. "Copy Path" in the right button menu of the thumbnails.

  • Keywords mangled as UTF-8 read as Shift_JIS (Windows Photo Gallery) put right, or left out.

  • The keywords of other programs (XMP, IPTC, the sidecar files "x.jpg.xmp" and "x.xmp") in the index: kept apart, shown and looked for as the tags put by hand of the same name.

  • With gexiv2 (Exiv2) the index reads EXIF, XMP and IPTC of HEIF, PNG, WebP, TIFF, raw files, ... (the date, the camera, the keywords).

  • With gexiv2 the EXIF of the formats other than JPEG too: Show EXIF Data... shows it, images and thumbnails are turned by its orientation.

  • "Scan EXIF Data..." is now "Show EXIF Data...".

  • Tools > Find Duplicates... rewritten: one search sorts them into Identical, Other Sizes and Similar; the groups on the left, their images side by side on the right to compare, each to keep or to delete (suggested for the identical and resized), and "Move to Trash..." for the group shown (a group at a time). It searches in a thread and reads the pictures itself.

  • File > Save Image... [Ctrl+S]: saves the image shown as JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, TIFF, BMP or PNM (a HEIC as a JPEG, ...), keeping its EXIF and XMP with gexiv2. New gdk-pixbuf image saver plugin.

  • Tags are a tree by "/" in the tag tab ("Travel/Kyoto" below "Travel"): a parent opens the images of the tags below it too; renaming a parent renames the tags below it.

  • The Data tab shows a tag with levels part by part, a parent part opens the tags below it; in Edit Tags a parent in "Tags in Use" types "Travel/" to pick from the tags below it.

  • A tag dragged onto another in the tag tab moves below it or merges into it.

  • The tags of the search window make a tree, a parent counting the tags below it.

  • [Edit | Keyboard Shortcuts...] to change the keys without editing the files.

  • The tags of people are a tree by "/" too (tag tab, people tab, search, data tab).

  • Edit the tags of a folder and the folders below it, from the directory view.

  • Manual: a new chapter "What Do You Want to Do?" (9) finds the way from what you want to do.

  • The same files in different folders: the index looks for them in the background ([Tools | Find Duplicates in the Index...]); all the groups to the trash at once, checked again first, the comments and bookmarks to the image kept, or links to it left in the places of the copies.

  • Update All Thumbnails works like loading: the thumbnails on the screen first (the scroll position is kept), the rest in the background without keeping them in memory; the progress (n/N) is shown and the stop button stops it. The preference "GUI redraw interval while loading" (and its tip) is gone.

  • Detail view: the title of the column sorted by shows the arrow (it was lost with every sort); "Image size" sorts by the area; columns with no sort are not clickable.

  • The thumbnail size in the toolbar can be typed in; Enter takes it and gives the keys back to the window (Shift+R, Ctrl+R).

  • The editing keys of a text field (Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C, Ctrl+X, Ctrl+V, Ctrl+Z, ...) go to the text field the focus is in, not to the menu keys of the window (Ctrl+A selected all the thumbnails while the location or the thumbnail size was edited).

  • Detail view: Ctrl+A (Select All) works with the focus in the list (the list took the key and did nothing).

  • Detail view: no "cb_sorter_changed: assertion" message on quit after sorting.

  • Open the Folder: the image chosen is shown also when the folder was not open yet (the top of the folder was shown).

  • Detail view: switching tabs (or closing one) keeps where the list of the tab was scrolled to (it went to the top).

  • Detail view: switching tabs puts the cursor (of the cursor keys) back on the image it was on if that is on screen, on the first image on screen otherwise (it was on the first image of the list).

  • Tabs of the thumbnails in a narrow place: the current tab keeps a width its name can be read in, the others a few characters (all were "..."); the close button on the current tab only, the middle button closes any tab; the current tab is lighter. "Show full path" is off by default (the full path is in the tooltip either way).

  • Movie player: a mute button and a volume slider at the right end of the control bar (GStreamer, MPlayer); the same for all the windows, kept after quitting.

  • Songs (m4a, mp3, flac, ogg, ...): the album art is shown while the song plays and is its thumbnail (GStreamer).

  • The "Data" tab shows the information of a song or a movie (title, artist, album, album artist, composer, track, disc, date, genre, comment, length, the formats of the sound and the picture; GStreamer, read in a thread; shown only).

  • The album art is shown at its own size (it was stretched, coarse), smaller only when the place is smaller; the zoom of images changes its size.

  • The seek bar of movies goes to the end (it stopped at 99 %: the time at the end was short of the length, e.g. 03:11 / 03:13).

  • Text viewer (plain text documents): a horizontal scroll bar for long lines (they could be read only by moving the cursor).

  • "Save Smaller..." in the right button menu of the thumbnails: saves the selected images smaller (800 px of height or less by default) as new files; what to add to the names ("_resized" by default: big_resized.jpg), the quality, keeping the shooting information, leaving out the location and copying the comments can be chosen. Saved in a thread, nothing is overwritten.

  • "Crop Image..." (C, [Edit] of the image window and the right button menus of the image): a frame on the image moved, resized or drawn anew, the ratio, "Detect the Edges" (what is inside the border of the color of the edges; the shadows of the scanner left out, a small margin), Enter saves name_crop.jpg next to the image (never over another file), Esc stops. Read, detected and saved in threads.

  • "Join Images..." in the right button menu of the thumbnails: the selected images one below another, side by side or in a grid, the width (height) made the one of the smallest, of the largest or not changed, saved as name_join.jpg next to the first one (never over another file). The overlaps of the pieces of one photo are looked for (a little different JPEG too) and can be put right for each seam, shown near its size (red triangles at both ends of the overlap, with a note on reading it), also as the difference. Read, detected and saved in threads.

  • "Open the Folder" and "Show the Saved Image": the image chosen is shown in the preview too (it was only selected).

  • Rename: only the name without the extension is edited, the extension stays (without it the file was no longer shown). "Change the extension too" edits the whole name; losing the extension of an image is asked about.

  • Detail view: the widths of the columns as the user left them stay after a rename, a sort or a refresh, in the other tabs and the next time (they went back to the defaults).

  • The album modes of the thumbnail view: Ctrl+F finds a file by its name, as in the detail view (it was [File | Open...] of the menu).

  • Switching to the detail mode no longer warns "Trying to measure GtkColumnViewCellWidget ... for width of 1" for each row (the column of the images is hidden, not 1 px wide).

  • Manual: the pictures taken again from the screens of now (the thumbnail window, the six display modes, the menus of the thumbnail window, [Edit] of the image window, the right button menus of the thumbnail view and the directory view, Edit Tags, 23 pages of the preferences).

  • Faces: the model files are checked (SHA-256): a broken download or another version of the same name is not used, and the preferences page and the status bar tell which file to download again and its URL. The index records the model the features of the faces were made with; with another one (a new version), the features are made again at the place of each face found, the faces and their names kept. "meson test faces" runs the models when they are there.

  • Duplicate images: in an Other Sizes group, a copy that is a smaller one only made larger (twice or more: predicted from the small one by a fitted enlarging) shows "Looks like an enlarged copy" and is not the one kept; it is looked for in a thread after the groups are shown.

  • A loader for the Pi format (PC-9801, X68000; 16 and 256 colours).

  • Archives: LZH archives of the -lh1- method of LHarc 1.x, which libarchive does not read, are unpacked. A file that could not be extracted is not tried again, and the error is told once for an archive (pressing OK brought the same message back again and again while the thumbnails were made).

  • File names not in the charset of the system (UTF-8), such as Shift_JIS names of the MS-DOS days, are shown as Shift_JIS, and renaming such a file no longer crashes; the new name is in UTF-8.

  • LZH archives with Shift_JIS names are read (libarchive could not convert the names and failed on the archive). A loader for MAKI01 (.mki, the format before MAG; PC-9801, 640 x 400 in 16 colours).

  • The user name and the machine in the header of a MAKI01 file are shown as comments of the file in the "Data" tab.

  • The comments in the file (MAG, MAKI, ...) are shown in the "Data" tab for an image in an archive too (read from the file extracted for the preview).

  • Fixed a crash on GTK 4.14 (Wayland) while drawing the thumbnail window when started without arguments: the CSS probes for the theme colors are registered at startup (PR #1 by Takuro Ashie).

  • File names that are not UTF-8, names in archives and comments in files are no longer taken as Shift_JIS. In a Japanese environment they are shown as the one of Shift_JIS, EUC-JP and ISO-2022-JP they read best in; bytes that cannot be read are shown as %XX.

  • File names shown in a charset guessed are in italics in the lists, and their tooltip and the status bar tell the charset and the bytes of the name. [Edit | File Names to UTF-8...] renames the names of a folder not in UTF-8 to UTF-8, checked first (nothing is overwritten).

  • With liblhasa, LZH archives are read with it: the old methods libarchive does not unpack (-lh1-, -lh4-, -lzs-, -lz5-, -pm1-, ...) and self-extracting archives (MS-DOS, X68000, ...) too. The files extracted are checked by their length and CRC.

  • Two pages: the preview and the image window show two pages side by side ([Two Pages] of the right button menu, T): right to left (manga, the default) or left to right, the first page alone, shift by one page (O), a wide page alone. A click goes on or back by the side clicked.

  • With two pages on, the status bar tells the pages shown ("Two Pages 2-3 / 8", "Two Pages 6 alone / 8"). The toolbar of the image window has a Two Pages button (the arrow on its right: the menu of two pages).

  • Layouts of pages side by side: pages side by side × rows (up to 4 × 3), right to left, the first page alone, under a name, chosen from [Two Pages | Page Layout]; "Two Pages (2 × 1)" and "Four Pages (2 × 2)" at first, [Two Pages | Edit Layouts...] to add, change, remove and order them. The status bar shows the name of the layout and the icon of the Two Pages button of the image window follows it.

  • With "Auto resize to image size" of the image window and pages side by side, the window is sized for all the pages of the layout (the cover alone too), when they are turned on, the layout is changed or another folder is shown, not at each page. Before, it took the size of one page.

  • Sort by name puts the numbers in names in the order of their value: 0p.jpg, 1.jpg, 2.jpg, 10.jpg (before, 0p.jpg came after 09.jpg and 10.jpg before 2.jpg). [View | Sort File List | Numbers by Value] turns it off. Clicking a column title of the detail view no longer resets "Case Insensitive" and "Mix Directories and Files".

  • Manual: the Tips are brought up to date (the tips about the bars and the window size of the image window that no longer apply are removed; the memory buffer, the thumbnail size, the custom layout rewritten).

  • Context menus open to the right of the pointer (no scroll bars).

  • Data tab: "Remove Item" for the item selected; "Delete Comment..." asks first and keeps the tags. The index follows a comment saved or deleted at once.

  • Slide show: the images after the first one are drawn again.

  • Corrected spelling and wording of the English messages and menus (Faild -> Failed, Buttton, Thumnail, Tool -> Tools, ...); old key bindings in ~/.gimv/keyaccelrc for renamed menu items are migrated. po/gimageview.pot regenerated (tools/po-update.py).

  • Dialog buttons (OK, Cancel, Apply, ...) are translated again.

  • Help > Document shows the (Japanese) documents in other locales too.

  • View > Layout and Window Composition show pictures of the layouts (a row of icon buttons; name and shortcut in the tooltip).

  • New GStreamer movie plugin: plays movies and audio in the image view (the formats depend on the installed GStreamer plugins) and makes movie thumbnails. It is the default player when no default view mode for movies is set.

  • Archives are read with libarchive instead of running lha, unzip, rar and tar; 7z, tar.xz and tar.zst are supported too, Shift_JIS file names in old zip/lha archives are converted.

  • Buttons use the icons of the desktop icon theme (Preferences > Common > Appearance); icon sets (iconset= in gimvrc, ~/.gimv/icons/<name>) may contain PNG and SVG files.

  • Tools > Set as Wallpaper sets the selected image as the desktop wallpaper (Xfce, GNOME, KDE Plasma, MATE, Cinnamon, LXDE/LXQt, feh; "This Desktop" picks the running one). Other formats and images in archives are converted to PNG in ~/.gimv/wallpaper.

  • The movie player shows the elapsed and the total time; a click on a playing movie no longer moves on to the next file.

  • Top-down BMP and CMYK JPEG files are loaded.

  • Printing (GTK print dialog): File > Print... (Ctrl+P) in the image window and the thumbnail window (selected images, one per page), and Print... in the popup menus. The "Image" tab of the print dialog chooses fit to the page or a resolution in dpi, turning the image to the page orientation, and printing the file name.

  • Movie seek bar: pointing at it shows the frame and the time there (GStreamer player; the time only for audio).

  • Transparent images are shown over a checkerboard (View > Checkerboard Behind Transparency, also in the popup menu and in Preferences > Image Window > Background); off blends them with the background colour as before.

  • Preferences > Movie and Audio: continuous play on/off.

  • Preferences > Movie and Audio: optionally ignore mouse actions for next/previous image, zoom, rotation and scrolling while a movie or audio file plays (off by default: the mouse settings apply).

  • Thumbnail view: directory names end with "/" in all view modes.

  • Movies and audio files in archives can be played (they are extracted first; before, the player reported "Resource not found").

  • Detail view: the image size is read from the file header (formats of gdk-pixbuf, and JPEG) instead of "Unknown" until the image was loaded; it is also updated when an image is opened. The sizes are read in the background after the list is shown.

  • The rotation of each image is remembered (kept in its comment file under ~/.gimv/comment, hidden in the comment editor) and restored when it is opened again; Preferences > Image Window > Image.

  • The HTML manual (Japanese) has screenshots, and its descriptions of the menus, toolbars, mouse menus, preferences and key bindings were changed to match this version.

  • Mouse button preferences: the side buttons "back" (button 8) and "forward" (button 9) can be assigned (nothing by default).

  • English manual (HTML) and English plain text documents in the Help menu; they are shown for every language that has no documents of its own (only Japanese has).

  • Color scheme (Preferences > Common > Appearance): the desktop theme, light or dark. With the desktop theme, a dark theme that GTK 4 cannot read (no gtk-4.0 directory, e.g. Greybird-dark) is shown with the dark Adwaita. Icons of GImageView drawn only with black lines are drawn with light lines in dark colors.

  • File open dialog: "Thumbnails of the selected files" also opens the selected folders, each in a tab of its own.

  • Image index (SQLite): gimv-indexer keeps the dates and tags of the images of the directories given in Preferences > Index, in the background. The "Date" and "Tag" tabs of the side notebook open them in a result tab; tags are edited from the thumbnail view and kept in the comment files, renamed and merged with F2; automatic tags (folder, camera, shape); desktop notices of new images; Tools > Missing Images... for the comments of images that went missing.

  • Faces and people (OpenCV 4.8, YuNet and SFace models): gimv-faces finds faces and groups them; the "People" tab, the faces window (naming, guesses, unnamed groups, tags of people) and the faces panel of the image view (P), where a face can also be added by hand. The names are kept in the comment files as "person/NAME" tags.

  • Tools > Search Images...: images of the index by tags and names (and, not), by the date taken, narrowed by the tags of the images found; searches can be kept by name.

  • Thumbnails: the freedesktop.org cache (~/.cache/thumbnails) is read and written; thumbnails are made in worker threads, from the EXIF thumbnail of a JPEG when it is big enough; they are turned and flipped by the EXIF orientation and by the rotation and flip the user gave the image.

  • Lists: Ctrl+F searches the rows (directory view, detail view, Data tab, preferences, index tabs, ...).

  • Status bar: the path, position and size of the focused image, or the number and total size of the images selected.

  • Data tab: the comments written in the image file itself (JPEG, PNG, GIF, MAG) are shown in italics, and the tags of the image.

  • Image view: Copy the Image to the Clipboard (Shift+Ctrl+C), Flip Horizontally (X, remembered with the rotation), Open the Folder.

  • Scrollbars are always shown (a preference, on by default).

  • GtkTreeView and the other API deprecated in GTK 4.10 are no longer used (GtkListView / GtkColumnView); GTK 4.12 and GLib 2.72 are required.
