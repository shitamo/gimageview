3. What Do You Want to Do?

This chapter finds the way to do things from what you want to do. Each gives the outline only; see the links for more. The index and face features need a build with them.

3.1. Getting the index ready

I want to find my images by date and tag

Add the folders of your pictures to "Directories to index" in [Preferences | Index]. The index is made while GImageView runs, and the "Date" and "Tag" tabs appear next to the directory view. The progress is shown at the right end of the status bar. This needs a build with SQLite.

See 2.6. Image index, [Preferences | Index].

I want to find my images by the people in them

Give the "Folders to find faces in" in [Preferences | Index | Faces] and put the model files in place. Faces are looked for after the index, and the "People" tab shows the faces that look alike in groups. This needs a build with OpenCV.

See 2.7. Faces and people, [Preferences | Index | Faces].

3.2. Tagging and arranging tags

I want to tag several images at once

Select the images in the thumbnail view: Ctrl+click adds one (or takes it away again), Shift+click selects all between, and a drag starting where there is no image selects the images in the rectangle; Ctrl+A selects all. Then choose "Edit Tags..." in the right button menu or [Edit | Edit Tags...], type a tag and press Enter; Enter with nothing typed is OK. The number selected is shown in the status bar ("5 selected").

See Tagging, Select All.

I want to tag all the images of a folder (and the folders in it)

Right-click the folder in the directory view and choose "Edit Tags with the Folders in It...". The folder need not be opened, nor the images selected. With many images the progress is shown, and "Stop" stops it.

See Tagging, the right button menu of the directory view.

I want to take a tag off several images

Select the images and open "Edit Tags...": a tag put on some of them only is shown as "Kyoto 2/5". × takes it off all of them, a click puts it on all of them; left alone, each image keeps what it has.

See Tagging.

I want to rename a tag, or merge two tags

Select the tag in the Tag tab and press F2, or choose "Rename..." in the right button menu. Giving a name that is there already asks whether to merge them. A tag dragged onto another one in the Tag tab can be merged too.

See Renaming and merging tags.

I want to arrange tags in a tree ("Travel/Kyoto")

A "/" in the name of a tag makes a tree. Drag a tag in the Tag tab onto the tag to be its parent and choose to move it below that tag. A click on a parent opens the images of the tags below it too.

See Tag tab.

3.3. Finding images

I want to see the images with a tag

Click the tag in the Tag tab: the images are shown in the result tab. The middle button opens a new tab. A tag in the "Data" tab of the comment window or the preview opens them too.

See Opening.

I want to find images by the day they were taken

Open the year, month and day in the Date tab and click it. For an image whose date is not known (an image of SNS, for example), a date written in the "Date" of the "Data" tab is used as its date.

See Date tab, 2.9. Other features.

I want to combine conditions ("Kyoto" without "Cat", for example)

[Tools | Search Images...] combines tags, people and the dates taken. A click on a tag adds it as a condition, and a click on the chip of a condition turns it into "excluded". Conditions used often can be kept under a name with "Keep...".

See 2.8. Searching images.

I want to open the folder of an image found

"Open the Folder" in the right button menu of the thumbnail view or the image view. A result tab shows the file names only; the full path is in the tooltip and the status bar.

See Opening.

3.4. People

I want the images of one person

Click the person (or an unnamed group) in the People tab. "Show the Faces..." of the right button menu opens the faces window to look at the faces.

See People tab.

I want to name faces, or take wrong faces away

Select faces in the faces window (Ctrl+A: all of them) and choose "Name...", "Not This Person", ... Sorting them "least like the person first" brings the wrong faces to the top. For one image, "Edit Faces (People)" (P) in the right button menu of the image view names them too.

See Faces window, Faces panel.

I want to add a face that was not found

Show the faces panel with "Edit Faces (People)" (P) in the right button menu of the image view, press "+" and drag over the face in the image.

See Faces panel.

3.5. Keeping images in order

I want to find duplicate images and clear them away

The same files in different folders: [Tools | Find Duplicates in the Index...] looks among all the indexed images (below a folder: the right button menu of the directory view; the same files as an image: "Find the Same Files" of the thumbnail view). Other sizes and similar pictures: open the images in a tab and choose [Tools | Find Duplicates...]. Check "Keep" and "Delete" and move them to the trash group by group (they can be restored from the trash), or all at once with "Move All Marked to Trash...". To keep the file names, "Trash and Leave Links" in the confirmation leaves a link to the image kept under each name.

See Find Duplicates in the Index..., Find Duplicates....

I want to put the tags and comments of a moved image back

Images moved with GImageView take their tags and comments with them. Images moved by other programs and not found any more are listed in [Tools | Missing Images...], where their comments can be put on where they went.

See Missing images.

I want to mark images to look at them again later

Select the images and choose "Bookmark..." in the right button menu; a note can be written. The "Bookmarks" tab (a star) next to the directory view opens them by the day they were bookmarked.

See 2.5. Bookmarks.

I want to add a note to a bookmark later

Click "Without a Note" in the Bookmarks tab (the star): the images without a note are shown in the result tab. Select one and choose "Bookmark..." of the right button menu (or [Edit | Bookmark...]) again: the field of the note comes. Write it and press Enter (what is written is kept too when the field is closed by a click elsewhere or Escape). A note there already is in the field, to be written again.

With several images selected, the note written goes to all of them (their notes before are replaced; closed with nothing written, each keeps its own).

See 2.5. Bookmarks, 6.3.5. The Bookmarks tab.

I want to open a folder or archive I look at often at once

Right-click the folder in the directory tab and choose "Bookmark..." (a folder or archive shown in the thumbnail view: "Bookmark..." in the right button menu, as an image). A click on it under "Folders and Archives" of the Bookmarks tab (a star) opens it.

See 2.5. Bookmarks.

A bookmarked image is "Not Found"

It was moved, renamed or deleted while GImageView was not running. A click on "Not Found" of the Bookmarks tab (a star) opens a list of them, with the files of the same name in the index as where they may have gone. "Put on Where It Went" or "Put on Another..." moves the bookmark with its note; "Remove the Bookmark" removes the one of a file deleted.

See 2.5. Bookmarks (moved and missing images).

I want to look at a part of a photo magnified (a loupe)

In the image window or the preview, press the left button with Ctrl and keep it pressed: a square of the image magnified comes at the pointer. It goes with the pointer, the wheel changes how much (100%, 200%, 400%...), and it goes when the button is released.

See 7.2.2. Mouse Button.

I want to save an image in another format (HEIC as JPEG, ...)

[File | Save Image...] (Ctrl+S) of the image window, or "Save Image..." in the right button menu of the preview, saves it in the format and quality chosen.

See Save Image....

I want to make photos smaller to save space

Select the images in the thumbnails and choose "Save Smaller..." in the right button menu. It saves them shrunk, for example to 800 px of height or less, as new files (the originals stay as they are). It can also leave out the location information.

See Save Smaller....

I want to join some images into one (a photo taken in pieces, ...)

Select them in the thumbnails and choose "Join Images..." in the right button menu: they are put one below another, side by side or in a grid and saved as a new file. When they are pieces of one photo that overlap, the overlaps are found and can be put right by hand.

See Join Images....

I want to take off the background around a scanned image

Show the image and press C (or "Crop Image..." in the right button menu), then "Detect the Edges" chooses what is inside the background around it (a border of one color, white, black, ...). Put the frame right by hand if needed and press Enter: it is saved as "name_crop.jpg" (the image stays as it is). Any part can be cropped by dragging the frame too.

See Crop Image....

3.6. Making it yours

I want to read a comic two pages at a time

Open the folder or the archive (zip, cbz, rar, lzh, ...), choose the first page and turn on [Two Pages | Two Pages] of the right button menu of the preview, or press T. Turn the pages with a click (the left half on, the right half back) or Space. When the pages do not pair, O shifts them by one. To show four pages at a time or in another layout, choose it from [Two Pages | Page Layout] ([Two Pages | Edit Layouts...] adds more).

See Two Pages.

I want to change the keyboard shortcuts

[Edit | Keyboard Shortcuts...]: click the key of a row and press the new key. The entry at the top narrows the list by name or key.

See Keyboard Shortcuts....

I want to find a name in a long list

Press Ctrl+F in the directory view, the Tag tab, the detail view, ... and type the name.

See Searching a list.
